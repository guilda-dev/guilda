Class: クラス: LocalController

Abstract base of controllers attached to one component, such as an AVR, a PSS, or an LQR. Observes its component by name and drives the inputs of the same name.

1 つの機器に付く制御器（AVR、PSS、LQR など）の抽象基底クラス。機器を名前で観測し、同名の入力を駆動。

Role役割

  • Owns its parameters, its equations (through its subclass), its equilibrium, and at most one nested controller (the PSS under an AVR).パラメータ、方程式（サブクラス経由）、平衡点、高々 1 つの入れ子の制御器（AVR の下の PSS）を保持
  • Parent: a component, or the AVR for a PSS.親は機器（PSS の場合は AVR）
  • No current output to the network. Outputs sv_y drive the parent's inputs of the same name; inputs sv_u read the observed component (the component above it, also through an AVR) by name. Rules of observing and driving: Class Design: Components and controllers.ネットワークへの電流出力なし。出力 sv_y は親の同名の入力を駆動、入力 sv_u は観測対象の機器（自分の上の機器。AVR を介する場合も含む）を名前で読み取り。観測と駆動の規則はクラス設計: 機器と制御器
  • Receives the parent voltage V in its functions, but does not own the voltage or the frequency, and is not a child of the bus.親の電圧 V は関数の引数として受け取り。ただし電圧・周波数は保持せず、母線の子でもない
  • Created with its constructor, attached with Component.add_local_controller.作成はコンストラクタ、接続は Component.add_local_controller
